I am a final-year undergraduate student in the Everest Class at UESTC, supervised by Prof. Zhao Kang. I will join the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yang Gao. I am also an intern at Spirit AI.

My research focuses on Embodied AI, especially robot learning and multimodal foundation models. I am also interested in representation learning and learning theory. My long-term goal is to build intelligent agents that can reason, learn, and act robustly in the physical world.
