About

I am a final-year undergraduate student in the Everest Class, UESTC, supervised by Prof.Zhao Kang. I'm also an intern at Spirit AI. I will join Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University this summer, advised by Prof.Yang Gao. My research focuses on Embodied AI, a frontier domain at the intersection of machine learning, robotics and computer vision. I'm also interested in Representation Learning and Learning theory. My research objective is to develop logically complete intelligent agents capable of fundamentally transforming human ways of life.
